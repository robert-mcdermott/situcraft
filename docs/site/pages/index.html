Spatial transcriptomics you can check

SituCraft opens the output of a 10x Genomics Xenium run, checks it, and shows the tissue, its transcripts and its cells at any zoom. It makes and compares cell segmentations, types and reviews the cells, brings in the H&E, asks how the tissue is organized, compares groups of subjects, and writes figures, methods and certificates that anyone can check by making the results again, keeping every decision on record, and an AI agent or a script can drive it in a window you watch. It runs on your own computer as one self-contained program, installed with a single command: no account, Python or R, and your data never leave your machine.

Why SituCraft

Checked from the bundle up

Every file read by SituCraft's own readers and checked against independent ones; each bundle checked before use, with 22 of the run's recorded values recomputed from its data; every image sample drawn exactly as recorded.

Segmentation you can question

Cells are the unit everything else counts. Make them again from the transcripts, compare any two segmentations cell by cell, see which cells' labels and which analyses the choice changes, and record the decision.

Private and self-contained

One program for macOS, Linux and Windows. Bundles are read where they are and never changed; nothing is uploaded, and the only request SituCraft makes is an example's download, when you ask.

On the record

Every result an artifact with its lineage, parameters, seed and checksums; the same settings give the same bytes on any computer; decisions and reviews appended, never removed; what made a result, and a methods section written from it with its references.

What it does

From the instrument's bundle to results others can check

Open a Xenium bundle, check it, look at the tissue, decide which cells to trust, name them, bring in the pathology, ask which cells are neighbors and where, compare groups of subjects, and write it up so that others can check it, each step kept on record.

Open and check

A bundle checked before it is used

SituCraft opens a Xenium output bundle as a folder, a zip or its experiment.xenium, says what it is and how far it is supported, and checks it: each finding fatal, a warning or a note, with the files it concerns, where in the tissue, and what to do.

  • 22 of the run's recorded values recomputed from its data
  • Thirteen broken bundles, each raising exactly its findings
  • Derived data built once and reused: image tiles, a transcript index, tiled outlines

The tissue and QC

Every sample as recorded, quality where it is

The Viewer draws the stains, the transcripts of up to 16 genes and the cells of any segmentation at any zoom, reading the data by tile. The QC view explains every metric the run recorded beside its recomputed value, maps quality over the tissue, and keeps cell filters as versions that never change the data.

  • Plots whose chosen cells light up in the tissue
  • Saved views and links that reopen exactly
  • Filters checked to keep the same cells whenever the project reopens
Quality control
The Lung run's metrics, recomputed from the data and reproduced.
Segmentations

Cells made, compared and decided

Expand the nuclei, import another tool's cells, or let SituCraft redraw each cell from its transcripts (after Jones et al. 2025), in a studio that says what a run needs before it starts. Any two segmentations are compared cell by cell, with measures of transcripts given to the wrong cell, and a workspace ranks their differences for review.

  • On simulated truth, 84.7% of transcripts in their true cell, against the instrument's 82.2%
  • Imports from SpatialData, Baysor, GeoJSON and Xenium Ranger
  • Exports to SpatialData, AnnData, Parquet, CSV, Matrix Market, GeoJSON and Xenium Ranger
  • Decisions kept with the comparison they rest on; no segmentation ever deleted
Comparing and deciding
Two segmentations of the Breast section with one camera, their differences ranked for review.
Cell types

Clusters, labels and their evidence

Cluster a segmentation's cells as scanpy does, see them on a map linked to the tissue, and label them from marker genes, from a labeled single-cell reference, or from a table made elsewhere. Labels are predictions: each cell's evidence is a click away, and a review queue ranks the cells most worth a look.

  • Starter marker sets for the examples' panels, from published atlases
  • Curated and consensus label sets as versions beside the predicted ones
  • The labels read under two segmentations: which cells the choice changes
Labels
The Breast cells' map beside the tissue, linked: the T cells selected on both.
The pathology bridge

The H&E on the tissue, placed in µm

Post-Xenium H&E and immunofluorescence images are drawn under the transcripts and cells, placed by Xenium Explorer's alignment, by landmarks, or automatically by their nuclei, and checked over the whole section in µm. Regions drawn on any image go to QuPath and back; reviews record each decision with what was on screen.

  • Alignments as versions, each with how well it fits and who made it
  • Regions in QuPath's GeoJSON, in the pixels of the image you open there
  • A review record as CSV or JSON, never changed
Images and alignment
The simulated H&E placed by its matrix, each checked patch colored by how far off it is.
Spatial analysis

How the tissue is organized

Graphs of which cells are neighbors; which cell types are neighbors more or less often than chance, and at what distances; what fills a region or gathers at its edge; the tissue's kinds of place (niches); the genes expressed in a pattern. Every number can show its cells on the tissue, and any answer can be made again on another segmentation to see how much it depends on the cells.

  • Enrichment, co-occurrence and Moran's I checked against Squidpy's
  • Ripley's K and L with the isotropic edge correction
  • A million cells' graphs, enrichment and niches in seconds on four cores
Spatial analysis
The Lung classes' neighborhood enrichment: neighbors found over expected, pair by pair.
Samples and cohorts

Groups compared with the subject as the unit

Bring a study's sections into one project, describe each sample, and state the design: the subject each came from, the groups, pairing and batches. The same steps run on every sample, and their cell types' abundances, genes and spatial statistics are compared between groups with each subject, not each cell, as one observation, every effect with its interval.

  • Expression's tests equal to limma's and edgeR's within 10⁻⁹
  • On 2,000 simulated null cohorts, each family's tests below 0.05 in 4.7% to 5.5% of them
  • Designs a batch could explain, or whose tests would be of cells, refused with why

Figures and a provable record

Results others can check

Figures are drawn from the project at a journal's column width, the tissue beside plots of results, each file carrying what it was drawn from. The methods are written from the record of every step. A certificate holds what a result rests on, and situcraft verify, or SituCraft itself, makes it again from the bundles and compares every file; a report or a package carries it to a reader, opens offline, and checks itself.

  • Figures read back by independent readers of SVG and PDF, the stains every pixel the suite's own composite
  • Certificates written on x86-64 made again on arm64, every file the same, bit for bit
  • Reports and packages opened offline: every number the certificate's, nothing left out found

Agents and scripts

An agent works in your window

An AI agent such as Claude Code asks SituCraft about your projects through situcraft mcp, a Model Context Protocol server: it reads results and their records, asks which cells meet conditions as a typed tree, never a query string, shows you the tissue and, with the leave you give it, runs the analyses, each change in the history under its name and every call in your library's log. Python and R packages call the same actions, and situcraft run makes any result again from its recipe, on a server or a cluster's nodes.

  • Every tool driven through situcraft mcp in CI, those that only read leaving the project as it was
  • Recipes made again without a window, every result with the same id, and verified
  • An agent benchmark graded by code against known answers

And

Built to be checked, taught and automated

Examples with known answers

Two public Xenium sections; a simulated tissue whose every cell, transcript and type is known, with an H&E and an immunofluorescence image of it; and three simulated cohorts with known effects, or none. Twenty-two worked examples, each a question answered step by step.

Scripts and notebooks

Python and R packages drive the open window by remote control, tables coming back as data frames and each action undoable in the project's history; situcraft run, refine and simulate work without it.

Whole sections

Refinements in patches of 55 million transcripts, 380,000 cells typed in minutes, a million cells' spatial analyses, and a cohort of 2 million cells summarized and compared, measured every week against stated budgets of time and memory.
